A more connected approach

Specialist knowledge. A more personal path forward.

Kent Healthcare is a specialist rehabilitation centre in Dubai supporting children and adults through a range of neurological, orthopaedic, developmental and related rehabilitation needs.

Its multidisciplinary approach brings different areas of clinical expertise together, helping patients work towards meaningful everyday goals through structured assessment and individualised care planning.

Rehabilitation should be shaped around the person, their needs and the progress they want to make.

The Kent approach

Care that considers the whole picture.

A considered rehabilitation journey connects clinical understanding with the practical goals that matter in daily life.

Understand

Begin with an individual clinical assessment and a clearer understanding of the person's needs.

Set meaningful goals

Build a structured plan around relevant functional, movement and everyday-life goals.

Coordinate care

Bring relevant clinical disciplines together when a patient's needs call for multidisciplinary support.

Support function

Focus on movement, communication, independence and participation in daily activities.

Track progress

Use documented goals and reassessment to review progress and inform the care plan.

Adapt as needed

Keep care responsive to the patient's changing needs, goals and clinical progress.
