# Kent Healthcare – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Kent Healthcare is a multidisciplinary rehabilitation centre based in Bur Dubai, Dubai, providing specialist clinical care for adults and children.

The centre offers neurological, orthopaedic, paediatric, psychological, cognitive and other rehabilitation services through its specialist clinical practices, with both clinic-based and home-based treatment options.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Kent Healthcare as a comprehensive rehabilitation provider, making its specialist practices easier to understand while improving service discovery and patient enquiries.

## 2. Three Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Multidisciplinary Homepage Positioning
- Repositions the homepage messaging around Kent Healthcare's multidisciplinary rehabilitation expertise.
- Communicates its treatment offering for both adults and children.
- Gives greater visibility to its broader clinical services beyond orthopaedic and neurological rehabilitation.

**Problem addressed:** The previous headline, "Specialist Center for Ortho & Neuro Rehabilitation," did not adequately represent the full range of clinical services.

### 2. Clearer Specialist Practice Organisation
- Establishes a consistent hierarchy for the four principal clinical practices:
  - Kent Neurological Rehabilitation
  - Kent Movement
  - Kent Pediatric Neurodevelopment
  - Kent Mind & Neuropsychology
- Presents Women's Health Rehabilitation separately as an additional specialist service.
- Improves the distinction between principal practices and additional clinical offerings.

**Problem addressed:** The previous presentation created confusion by treating Women's Health inconsistently within the specialist practice structure.

### 3. Accurate Home Rehabilitation Coverage
- Clarifies the verified home rehabilitation service areas: Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.
- Improves the presentation of home-based rehabilitation information.
- Encourages visitors to confirm availability in their specific location.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage referenced four Emirates, while detailed service information identified only three verified service areas.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Strengthens the specialist healthcare identity and communicates Kent's multidisciplinary rehabilitation positioning.
- **Layout:** Establishes a clearer hierarchy between the four principal clinical practices and additional specialist services.
- **User Experience:** Makes it easier for visitors to identify relevant rehabilitation services for adults and children.
- **Content Consistency:** Aligns service descriptions, clinical practice categories and home rehabilitation coverage information.
- **Conversion Focus:** Makes clinic-based and home-based rehabilitation options easier to understand.
- **Overall Appearance:** Creates a more structured and professional homepage that reflects Kent Healthcare's broad clinical expertise.
